## Video direction

Use deep-space navy as the canvas, cyan as the primary signal color, violet for intelligence and context, lime for healthy local execution, and orange for safety. Typography is large and editorial, with high contrast, restrained glass panels, and precise grid lines. Motion uses deterministic GSAP transforms and opacity only; all scene timing is declared on the HTML clips.
